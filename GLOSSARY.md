# WHFD CTF research

Terms used when designing research into AI assistance during CTF participation.

## Language

**Challenge**:
A participant-facing CTF task, including its description, supplied artifacts and challenge service behavior.

**AI-use signal**:
An observation that may help investigate AI assistance, with its source and limitations. A signal alone is not a verified label of AI use.
_Avoid_: Proof of AI, AI verdict

**Research observation**:
A recorded action or property of a participant's interaction with the hosted CTF, used to study AI assistance. Research observations do not determine competition penalties.
